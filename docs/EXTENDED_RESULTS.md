# Extended results

The repository is slightly broader than the planned Letter. It retains:

- five state-generation families and three probe ensembles;
- rank-2, rank-4, and rank-8 spectrum interventions;
- the general locally dressed four-purity theorem;
- random-Pauli and independent-seed boundary-code analyses;
- alternative finite-size corrections and time-window checks;
- transition-context analysis;
- state-vector/tableau validation.

These support the central chain but are not separate headline claims. See `figures/extended/` and the two study directories.
