# Notation

- $m=n/2$: central cut.
- $P_j=\operatorname{Tr}\rho_{[1:j]}^2$: purity across cut $j$.
- $S_j$: stabilizer entropy across cut $j$.
- $d=2^{n/2}$: half-chain Hilbert-space dimension.
- $\chi_{\mathrm{rel}}=\frac{d}{d-1}(1-\mathbb E P_m'/P_m)$: relative Renyi-2 entangling susceptibility.
- $\delta_L=S_m-S_{m-1}$ and $\delta_R=S_m-S_{m+1}$: boundary code.
- $\beta_n=\partial\chi_{\mathrm{rel}}/\partial(p/0.02)$ at fixed $(n,\tau,S_m)$.
