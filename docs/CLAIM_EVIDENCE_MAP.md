# Claim–evidence map

The machine-readable ledgers are retained under the two study directories. The main claims are:

1. **Central-spectrum insufficiency.** Supported by held-out rank-4 intervention, independent-seed replication, five state-generation families, and additional rank/probe checks.
2. **Exact neighboring-cut mechanism.** Established by the local-twirl response theorem and its Haar/Clifford specialization.
3. **Physical exact-spectrum persistence.** Supported by monitored stabilizer states matched in $(n,\tau,S_m)$ strata through $n=256$.
4. **Independent replication.** Supported by a disjoint seed and random-Pauli monitoring.
5. **Paired causal localization.** Supported by varying measurement position on copies of the same premeasurement state, conditional on $\Delta S_m=0$.

The repository excludes order-parameter, critical-exponent, and universal non-Clifford thermodynamic claims.
