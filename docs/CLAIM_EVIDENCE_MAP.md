## Interpretations that need additional assumptions

The limiting coefficient is an extrapolation. All checked finite-size models retain negative intercept intervals, but the model menu is finite and the primary random-Pauli inverse-size residual is substantial. Neither a rigorous thermodynamic limit nor exhaustive model uncertainty is established.

The historical exponential parameter near 2.37 sites does not establish a physical localization length. The full distance profile is poorly described by a single exponential even after covariance weighting and common eligibility. The defensible empirical statement is a response concentrated near the cut and rapidly attenuating over the measured distances. It is not a theorem of finite support.
