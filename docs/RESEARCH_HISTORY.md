# Research history and rejected branches

The project began with entanglement-trajectory fingerprints and later a cumulative spectral-flow-balance interpretation. Publication-level audits changed the central story.

## Rejected: generic trajectory superiority

Entropy-only ablations did not support the broad claim that trajectory features generally outperform ordinary entanglement summaries.

## Rejected: cumulative flow balance as a transition principle

For any bounded coordinate $X$, splitting a step into unitary and measurement increments gives

$$\Delta X_U(t)+\Delta X_M(t)=X_{t+1}-X_t.$$

The cumulative cancellation ratio therefore approaches one in a stationary regime by telescoping. Near-cancellation is not, by itself, transition-specific.

## Surviving direction

A non-telescoping question remained: after controlling the central spectrum, what determines the response to the next fresh gate? That question produced the exact neighboring-purity mechanism, physical stabilizer matching, large-size replication, and paired location intervention.

Historical split-flow and Aubry–André archives are intentionally not part of the active workflow.
