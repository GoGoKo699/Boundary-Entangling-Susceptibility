# Figure 1 caption

Complete central-spectrum control leaves a response difference. Filled circles show the held-out primary run; open squares show the independent-seed run. Points are the unchanged equal-cell high-minus-low contrasts at monitoring probabilities 0.24 and 0.08, evaluated after rank-4 spectrum equalization within each family, size, and time cell. Bars are nominal pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 50,000 valid support-conditioned trajectory-cluster bootstrap replicates per contrast. Whole trajectories are resampled within size/rate strata, retaining their time records and eligibility masks together. The primary pool includes trajectories with at least one eligible retained-time row; a whole proposal is rejected if any required cell is empty. Archived run-specific reference spectra and the observed common support are held fixed (four cells for Clifford, six for each other family). These conditional, post-hoc intervals do not include reference- or support-selection uncertainty and are not simultaneous intervals. Spectrum replacement is a diagnostic operation, not an experimental protocol.
