## Which reproduction task do you need?

A figure redraw reads a canonical table. A record replay recalculates statistics from stored observations. A bootstrap regeneration draws new, reproducible resamples under the specified recipe. A fresh simulation campaign generates new physical trajectories. These are different tasks, and a successful check of one does not establish the others.

The [reproduction guide](../../docs/REPRODUCTION.md) gives separate commands. The [reproducibility limits](../../docs/REPRODUCIBILITY_LIMITS.md) distinguish the current, regenerable Figure 1 uncertainty from its unrecovered historical bootstrap, and original stored resamples from later sensitivity calculations.
