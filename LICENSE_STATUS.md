## Material scope

These licenses apply to original material in the repository tree carrying this notice, to the extent Ruge Lin holds the relevant rights. They apply to different materials; they are not a choice of either license for every file. The complete standard texts linked above control; this page identifies their scope without modifying their terms.

MIT allows use, modification and redistribution, including commercial use, while requiring retention of its copyright and permission notice. CC BY 4.0 also allows commercial reuse and adaptation; its terms include attribution, a license link and identification of changes. Compliant recipients retain the CC permissions once granted. Neither license requires downstream work to use the same license.

The citation in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) supports scholarly credit and reproducibility. It adds no mandatory paper-citation clause to MIT and imposes no extra CC restriction. For CC material, identify Ruge Lin, retain supplied attribution and notices, link to this repository and the license, and indicate modifications as required by the license. An example credit is: “Ruge Lin, Beyond the Schmidt Spectrum, [repository](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Boundary-Entangling-Susceptibility), CC BY 4.0; adapted.” Use the actual modification status and identify the commit or release used for reproducibility.
